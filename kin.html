Haabʼ calculation settings

Gran Wayebʼ mode replaces the regular five-day Wayebʼ at the end of each 52-Haab block with a 13-day Gran Wayebʼ numbered 0–12. The supplied count anchors 2013-01-02 as 0 Gran Wayebʼ. The standard option preserves the classical 365-day Haabʼ cycle.
